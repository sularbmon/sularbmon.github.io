Hello! I’m Su, a computer vision and machine learning engineer based in Tokyo. I build AI systems that connect research with real-world applications—from collecting and preparing datasets to training models, evaluating performance, and deploying systems on edge devices.

I am currently a Computer Vision / AI Engineer at AIRUCA Inc., where I work on end-to-end machine learning systems for industrial applications. Previously, through Human Resocia, I contributed to multimodal human–robot interaction research at Honda Research Institute Japan. I hold a Master of Engineering from the University of Miyazaki, where I researched video-based cattle identification and tracking.

I enjoy bridging academic research and production engineering—turning ideas into reliable systems that can operate with real cameras, changing environments, and constrained hardware.
